## In Memoriam

Jeff Pasnak, credited as **J.P. Pasnak, CD (Linegod)**, was a longtime core
contributor. Cacti's [1.2.31 release announcement][release] records his
passing and honors his contributions to the project and community.

## Community Contributions

Cacti depends on contributors who write and review code, maintain packages,
improve documentation, translate the interface, report security issues, test
releases, and help other users.

See the maintained [Cacti contributor credits][contributors],
[code contribution history][code-history], and
[documentation contribution history][docs-history] for broader recognition.
Translation contributions are also recorded on the
[Cacti translation platform](https://translate.cacti.net/).
